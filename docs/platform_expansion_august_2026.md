# Sports Terminal platform expansion — August 2026

This branch turns the current NBA-first product into a full-page, linked research and community platform. It is intentionally implemented as a coherent product architecture rather than disconnected mock screens.

Primary goals:

- one outer platform scroll surface; major product pages do not create competing vertical scroll regions
- basic NBA Stats page plus a separate Advanced Stats destination containing the full metric/research workstation
- first-class linked player and team pages from every new stats/NBA Hub surface
- expanded NBA Hub with league overview, players, teams, schedule/results, standings, awards/honors, leaders, transactions and saved context
- deeper trade machine with 2–5 teams, players/picks/exceptions/cash, cap/apron diagnostics, roster math, trade value notes, scenario persistence and sharing metadata
- awards/honors index built around annual awards, voting, All-NBA/All-Defense/All-Rookie teams, All-Star honors and special awards
- full editorial, team blog, community, profile, Python notebook and legal/information surfaces
- mandatory legal acceptance before account creation/session use, with versioned acceptance metadata

The legal text in this branch is a product-ready comprehensive draft, not a substitute for review by counsel before a public launch. Jurisdiction-specific rights, arbitration/class-waiver language, governing law, entity identity, registered address, privacy request channels, subprocessors, data-retention periods and launch-state regulatory scope must be finalized before production.
